Home Chores Digital Assistant

A camera looks at each room on a schedule. A local model works out what's actually out of place — a cutting board mid-use is not clutter — scores the room, compares it to yesterday, and hands you a short list: what to do, where it is, roughly how long it'll take. It has opinions about laundry.

How it actually works

Five stages. Everything before the last one happens on a machine in the cupboard.

01
Capture

A still per room on a schedule. No continuous recording, ever.

02
Detect

Find and label what's in the frame, and where it sits.

03
Judge

Decide what counts as out of place, and how badly.

04
Compare

Today against yesterday: what's new, what got dealt with.

05
Tell you

A short list on your phone. Text only — never images.

The one hard rule

Frames never leave the house.

A camera pointed at your kitchen is a bigger ask than a gadget that beeps. So the rule is in the architecture, not in a settings toggle: pictures are processed and deleted on the machine that took them. What persists is text — labels, scores, task history. Notifications carry text only.

✓ Processing on our own hardware
✓ Text-only notifications
✓ No cameras in bedrooms. Not optional.
